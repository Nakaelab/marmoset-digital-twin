Abstract

The common marmoset (Callithrix jacchus) is an important non-human primate model in neuroscience and biomedical research. However, existing 3D resources are primarily limited to brain atlases or keypoint-based pose estimation, and reusable data resources for integrative handling of body surfaces, fur, joint structures, and experimental environments have not been sufficiently developed. In this study, we constructed a multimodal dataset for three-dimensional reconstruction of common marmoset body–environment interactions.

The dataset includes: a whole-body surface mesh based on CT imaging; fur representation based on photographic references; a rigged 3D model for pose-driven animation; synchronized behavioral videos of three individuals (~90 hours total) from 8 viewpoints; 2D/3D keypoint estimation data; a 3D experimental environment model based on blueprint information; and pseudo-first-person perspective renderings integrating pose estimation results with the 3D model.

Technical validation evaluated geometric agreement between the CT-derived mesh and surface model, accuracy of 2D/3D keypoint estimation, dimensional accuracy of the environment model, and structural similarity between real camera images and digital twin renderings. This dataset provides a foundation for treating marmoset natural behavior not merely as point sequences, but as three-dimensional phenomena including the relationship between body shape and environment, enabling applications in behavioral analysis, visualization, synthetic data generation, and future digital twin research.

Dataset Contents

Browse & Download Dataset

🦴 3D Marmoset Body Data

  • CT scan data (.nii)
  • Whole-body segmentation (.nii.gz, .stl)
  • CT-derived surface mesh (Marmoset_surface.stl)
  • Rigged 3D model with 13 IK bones and HairCurves fur (Blender v4.5.0)

🎥 Multi-view Behavioral Video

  • 3 individuals: cj611 (55.46 h), cj425 (33.38 h), cj357 (1.33 h)
  • 8 synchronized cameras (Basler acA2040-35gc, 2048×1536, 24 fps)
  • ~90 hours total behavioral recording

📐 2D/3D Keypoint Estimation

  • 20 anatomical keypoints (nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, tail)
  • YOLOX detection + HRNet-w48-DEKR pose estimation
  • 3D reconstruction via Hungarian algorithm + anipose smoothing
  • Trained model weights provided (.pth)

🏗️ 3D Environment & Digital Twin

  • Full experimental cage and room model in Blender
  • Blueprint-based reconstruction with Python alignment scripts
  • Integrated digital twin animation keyframes per session
  • Pseudo first-person perspective renderings (FOV: ±100° H, ±70° V)
